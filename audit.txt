Web Portfolio Design and Accessibility Audit

Task 1: Design and Usability Audit

1. Is my navigation menu clear and easy to find on every page?
   Yes. The navigation menu is clearly displayed on every page and provides links to Home, Projects, and Contact.

2. Is there a clear visual hierarchy?
   Yes. The h1 headings are larger than the h2 headings, making the most important headings easier to recognize.

3. Is the text easy to read?
   Yes. The website uses readable font sizes, spacing, and good contrast between the text and background.

Task 2: Accessibility Check

1. Image Alt Attributes
   Yes. I checked all images and made sure every image has a descriptive alt attribute.

2. Contact Form Labels
   Yes. I checked the contact form and made sure each label's for attribute matches the id of its input or textarea.
